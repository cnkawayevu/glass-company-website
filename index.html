Architectural Glass,

Craft Expansion Glazing specializes in precision glass installations, aluminium fabrication, and bespoke architectural solutions for residential, commercial, and institutional projects across Ghana.

From frameless shower cubicles to full-facade curtain wall systems, every installation is engineered to exact tolerances — because we believe the quality of the material must match the integrity of the craft.
